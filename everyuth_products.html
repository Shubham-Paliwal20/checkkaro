Product Index — Everyuth Naturals

Everyuth Naturals is a mass-market Indian skincare brand by Zydus Wellness (formerly Zydus Cadila). Known for affordable face washes, scrubs, and peel-off masks sold across Indian pharmacies and supermarkets. Brand claims "nature-powered" formulas.
⚠ Important Note on Ingredient Transparency: Everyuth's official website does NOT publish full INCI ingredient lists — only marketing "key ingredients" are disclosed. Full INCI data was sourced from INCIDecoder (international ingredient database) where available. Products marked Key Only have no publicly declared full INCI list — these are shown with known ingredients only and cannot be fully graded. Grades are based only on confirmed INCI lists.

📊 Everyuth Naturals — Brand Summary & Recommendations

All Confirmed Products: Grade D
Every product with a confirmed full INCI (4/10) scored Grade D. Common concerns across all:

🚩 Parabens (Methyl, Propyl, Butyl, Ethyl, Isobutyl)
🚩 Methylisothiazolinone (MIT) — EU-restricted allergen
🚩 Triclosan (Walnut Scrub — EU/FDA banned)
⚠ SLES or SLS as primary surfactant
⚠ Triethanolamine — nitrosamine formation risk
⚠ Propylene Glycol
⚠ Cyclopentasiloxane (D5) (Tulsi Turmeric FW)
What Everyuth Does Right:
• Affordable price point makes skincare accessible
• Botanical actives (Neem, Tulsi, Turmeric, Aloe) are genuine and effective
• Niacinamide + Salicylic Acid in Tulsi Turmeric FW are well-dosed actives
• Laminaria Digitata (marine algae), Vitamin E, C, A in most products

Better Alternatives to Consider:
For similar price range with cleaner preservatives: Minimalist, The Derma Co., Dot & Key, Plum, Mamaearth (check INCI). For Neem/Tulsi face wash: look for MIT-free, paraben-free options.
Note on Grades for Indian Market Context: Parabens are still permitted by the CDSCO / Drugs & Cosmetics Act in India. Grade D reflects the international SCCS / EWG standard used by CheckKaro. Everyuth products are not illegal in India — they are legal, widely sold products. The grading helps consumers make informed decisions when choosing between products.
