About HerbsLand

Brand Overview

HerbsLand® is an Indian brand available on Amazon.in that sells 100% pure bio-organic herbal powders for hair care and skin/face care. All products are SLS-free, paraben-free, preservative-free, and chemical-free.

Product Format

Products are sold as combo packs, where each sachet contains one type of pure herb powder, double-filtered for fineness. Each sachet can be mixed with water, yogurt, milk, or oil to create hair packs and face masks.

Hair Care Range

Amla, Reetha, Shikakai, Bhringraj, Hibiscus, Henna, and Neem powders — used as a natural alternative to chemical shampoos, conditioners, and hair masks.

Skin/Face Care Range

Multani Mitti (Fuller's Earth), Sandalwood, Orange Peel, Mulethi, Rose Petal, and Wild Kasturi Turmeric — used for DIY face packs, skin brightening, and anti-acne treatments.

✅ Why All HerbsLand Products Grade A

HerbsLand products contain only 100% pure, dried, and powdered plant materials — no synthetic chemicals, preservatives, surfactants, or additives of any kind. Every ingredient is a well-recognised traditional herb used in Ayurveda for centuries. None are classified as "commonly questioned" or "worth knowing" in our ingredient database, which is why every product achieves a Grade A.

⚠️ Important Usage Notes

Henna (Lawsonia Inermis): Pure henna is safe, but always do a patch test — allergic reactions are possible, especially if previously exposed to PPD (black henna). Multani Mitti: Can be drying — limit to 1–2 times/week. Avoid if you have very dry skin. Wild Kasturi Turmeric (Curcuma Aromatica): May temporarily stain skin yellow. Not the same as dietary turmeric.

🧪 How to Use HerbsLand Herbal Powders

For Hair (Natural Shampoo):
Mix Amla + Reetha + Shikakai powders with warm water into a paste. Apply on scalp and hair, massage gently, leave 10 min, rinse. Use 2–3x per week. Initial 2–3 weeks may show more hair fall as residual chemicals leave — this is normal.
For Hair (Deep Conditioning Mask):
Mix Bhringraj + Hibiscus with coconut oil or yogurt. Apply to scalp and lengths, leave 30–60 min under a towel, rinse with natural shampoo. Use 1–2x per week.
For Face (Face Mask):
Take 1–2 tsp of your chosen powder(s). Mix with rose water, plain water, yogurt, honey, or milk depending on skin type. Apply evenly, avoid eye area, leave 10–15 min, rinse gently. Pat dry. Use 1–2x per week.
Storage Tips:
Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight. Do not introduce wet spoons into the powder packet. Shelf life: 12–24 months when stored properly. No preservatives — keep sachets sealed when not in use.
